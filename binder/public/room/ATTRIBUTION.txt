The city combines adapted Project PLATEAU building geometry, an authored
lattice tower, temple geometry, foliage, roads, original facade/interior/sign
details, and licensed PBR surface scans. PLATEAU attribution and changes are
documented in ../city/ATTRIBUTION.txt. No photographic skyline is rendered.
The combined scene is an art-directed interpretation, not a surveyed 1:1
reconstruction. The temple reuses the original procedural roof/temple kit
already in this repository. Room geometry, procedural surface details,
contact-shadow textures, shop signs, and sakura artwork are original to this
prototype. User-provided reference photos and illustrations are not embedded.
