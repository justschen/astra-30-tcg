This is an independent adaptation, not an official MLIT visualization.
Changes made by Afterhours:
- Selected a bounded subset of the Minato 2025 LOD3 tileset. Individual
  buildings retain their actual source level: LOD1, LOD2 or LOD3.
- Converted ECEF coordinates to a scaled local frame, flattened building
  foundations to the authored ground, and omitted volumes that interfere
  with the original park, animated streets or Tokyo Tower composition.
- Repositioned and proportionally fitted 27 selected source buildings to
  the existing foreground composition; these are not their GIS locations.
- Welded and quantized geometry, compressed local buffers, resized and
  repacked texture atlases, and derived approximate glass masks.
- Rebuilt close-range cladding, glazing, window interiors, frames and
  rooftop equipment. These details and all night illumination are
  authored approximations, not surveyed appearance or live occupancy.
- Seven foreground buildings now use independently modeled,
  reference-inspired silhouettes instead of the selected survey meshes.
  Their unused source data remains in the package for reproducibility.
- Additional near-district infill buildings, parking/service courts and
  their contents are authored additions, not part of the survey data.

manifest.json records the exact source URL and SHA-256 of every imported
tile. No city panorama or skyline photograph is used as a backdrop.

The separate tower, temple, roads, traffic, shop signs, plants and room
remain authored geometry. CC0 surface-map sources are credited in
../room/ATTRIBUTION.txt. This credit does not imply source endorsement.
