    MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

    Your quiet corner.

    A virtual binder, a few stacks, and the city outside.

    Room controls

    Drag right to move the scene right, or left to move it left. Drag down to look up; drag up to look down. Page turns use the arrow keys or a page-edge drag.

    The top overlays and save status fade after 10 seconds of inactivity. Move, tap, or press a key to bring them back. Menus stay open while you use them.

    Television

    Watch the requested video on the right wall. YouTube is contacted only when you play it; playback starts muted.

    Graphics and performance

    Balanced uses a steady render resolution while moving, placing cards, and resting, so the scene does not shift when you stop. Full detail uses more pixels; Smooth uses fewer. All modes reuse unchanged room shading. Card artwork, buildings, traffic, and people are never removed.

    Pick up cards
    Click cards in your binder, bag, or table stacks. No dragging needed. The hand grows left and wraps; Fold hand keeps only the selected card visible.
    Select and place
    Click a hand card to select it, then click a highlighted pocket or stack label. Clear hand returns all held cards to the bag in one undoable action.
    Inspect
    Double-click or right-click a hand card, or press E to inspect the selected card. Bag cards also have an eye button.
    Admire
    A holds up the selected card. Drag left/right to turn all the way around, up/down to tilt, or use the arrow keys and buttons. The card stays upright. Flip shows the other side; Reset restores the front. Esc returns to your hand.
    Lift and turn
    Lift binder holds the actual book upright. ← → and mouse page-edge turns work both in hand and on the table. Drag the room to look; scroll to zoom.
    Your bag
    B opens your bag. On table includes a selector for To sort, Keepers, and Trades. Place cards returns to the current binder view.
    Change your mind
    Delete puts back the selected card. Esc closes a menu or returns unplaced cards to their saved locations. Ctrl / Cmd + Z restores the last action and selection.
    Keyboard collection
    Pocket view uses standard buttons and a card selector. Tab to a card or pocket and press Enter. It also works without WebGL.

    Outside your window

    Rain adds an overcast storm layer and thicker haze. Clear restores your chosen cloud level.

    Moving traffic: up to 768 cars. Parked cars stay in their neighborhood courts.

    Traffic, aircraft, rooftop lights, and weather move independently.

    A city with depth

    The neighborhood combines real building meshes with original tower, temple, streets, shops and greenery. Seven distinctive skyline anchors are individually modeled from the reference composition. Tokyo Tower has a flared truss base, two observation decks and a segmented mast. Nearby facades have rebuilt cladding and window frames; distant buildings retain survey textures. Height-dependent haze adds atmospheric depth. Lighting, occupancy and reflections are simulated, not live or ray traced. The combined scene is art-directed, not an exact GIS replica.
